Privacy Policy

Extension: Decoder - Ad Library Research. Last updated: 24 October 2026.

This privacy policy explains what the Decoder - Ad Library Research browser extension ("the extension") does with information when you use it. The extension is published by an independent developer and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Meta Platforms, Inc.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your browsing history.
  • We do not collect your name, address, phone number, financial information, health information, or any government identification.
  • We do not collect passwords, authentication cookies, or credentials for any website.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.
  • We do not use your data for any purpose unrelated to the single feature of the extension.

Information the extension processes

The extension works on the publicly available Meta Ad Library website. When you open an Ad Library page, the extension reads the advertising information that is already shown publicly on that page - for example ad creatives, advertiser names, run dates, and the public reach and impression figures that Meta itself publishes. This information is used to calculate and display estimated ad spend and related research insights inside the page you are already viewing.

This processing happens in your own browser. The extension does not read pages on other websites.

Optional account and settings

If the extension offers an optional sign-in or saved-settings feature, the limited data needed for it - such as your email address and your own saved preferences or saved searches - is stored on our hosted database provider (Supabase) solely to operate that feature. This data is used only to let you sign in and to keep your settings available to you. It is not sold or shared for advertising. If you do not sign in, no such data is stored.

Local storage

The extension may store settings and cached results locally in your browser using the standard storage APIs. You can clear this at any time by removing the extension or clearing your browser data.

Permissions

The extension requests only the permissions required for the feature described above. Host access is limited to Meta Ad Library pages so that the extension can read the public ad information displayed there and show its analysis on the same page.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data is removed when you uninstall the extension or clear your browser storage. If you created an optional account, you can request deletion of that account and its associated data by contacting us using the link below, and it will be deleted.

Children

The extension is intended for business and marketing research use and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

For any privacy question or a data deletion request, please open a request here: https://github.com/globalalphanifty-byte/decoder-privacy/issues
